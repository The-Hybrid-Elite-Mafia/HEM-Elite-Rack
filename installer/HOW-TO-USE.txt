HEM-ELITE-RACK — HOW TO USE

1. Start HEM-Elite-Rack (desktop shortcut).
2. OPEN SONG (top) — or drag a WAV / MP3 / FLAC onto the window.
   Songs at other sample rates are converted to 48 kHz when they load.
3. + ADD PLUG-IN (left) — pick any plug-in (tabs: EQ, DYNAMICS, COLOR, SPACE, MOVEMENT, TOOLS). Up to 8 plug-ins, top to bottom.
   Each slot: ON / OFF, UP / DOWN (order), DEL (remove). Click a slot to show its faceplate.
4. PLAY (or the SPACE bar). Click the song strip to jump; LEFT / RIGHT arrows jump 5 s.
5. A PROCESSED / B ORIGINAL (or the A and B keys) — compare with and without the chain.
   MATCH LOUDNESS (on by default) brings B to the same loudness as A, so you judge the
   sound, not the volume.
6. EXPORT WAV — the whole song through the chain, 24-bit / 48 kHz. The status line then
   shows the file's integrated loudness (LUFS) and peak.

SURGEON
  Double-click the graph to add a band where you clicked. Drag a dot: frequency and gain.
  Mouse wheel on a dot: width (Q). Double-click a dot: band off. Bands 1-16 buttons pick a
  band; its TYPE, STEREO (stereo / mid / side / left / right), SLOPE, FREQ, GAIN, Q are
  under the graph. DYNAMIC: the band only moves (up to RANGE) when that part of the song
  goes over THRESHOLD. CHARACTER: CLEAN, CONSOLE or TUBE colour, with DRIVE.
  AUTO GAIN keeps the loudness where it was so louder never fools you.

THE CLEANER
  NOISE REDUCTION: how hard. FOCUS: lean on the lows (hum, rumble) or the highs (hiss).
  AIR PRESERVE: protects the top end and the attacks. FREQUENCY RANGE: drag the two
  handles to work only between them. ADAPT: keeps learning the noise (off = freeze it).
  LISTEN: hear only what is being taken out.

THE FAMILY
  A mix-bus compressor: a few dB on the needle pulls a mix together. THRESHOLD, RATIO (2, 4,
  10:1), ATTACK, RELEASE (AUTO waits longer after the mix has been working it hard), MAKEUP,
  MIX (lower it for parallel compression), SIDECHAIN HPF (60 / 90 / 150 Hz: the kick and bass
  stop making the whole mix duck).

ARCHIVE
  INPUT drives the tape (AUTO TRIM keeps the level the same, so you hear the colour, not the
  volume). SPEED 15 IPS = more low-end bump and a softer top, 30 IPS = tighter and brighter.
  HEAD BUMP, WOW (pitch drift), HISS. VU CAL picks what reads 0 on the meters: -18 dBFS for quiet
  mixes, -10 (the default) or -6 for loud finished songs.

VELVET
  LOW: pick a frequency, BOOST and/or ATTEN. Both together = the classic trick: more weight at
  the very bottom and less mud just above. HIGH: frequency, BOOST, WIDTH (sharp .. broad).
  HIGH ATTENUATION: SELECT 5 / 10 / 20 kHz and ATTEN to soften the top. TUBE: DRIVE, OUTPUT.

LOCKDOWN (put it last in the chain)
  INPUT pushes the song into it: louder. CEILING is the most that ever comes out (-1 dB is a
  safe choice for streaming). TRUE PEAK on keeps the peaks *between* samples under the ceiling
  too. RELEASE (or AUTO), LINK. The readouts show the loudness (LUFS) and the true peak of what
  comes out; the true peak turns red if it is over the ceiling.

WIDE LOAD
  WIDTH 100 % = as it was, 0 % = mono, up to 200 %. MONO BASS keeps the low end in the centre
  (punchier, safe on club systems and phones). CORRELATION: +1 = mono, 0 = wide,
  below 0 = parts cancel in mono (be careful).

SPARK
  FREQUENCY = above where new harmonics are added; nothing below it changes. TUBE is warmer,
  TAPE edgier. DRIVE = how much grit, MIX = how much is added. A little goes a long way.

ENFORCER
  The all-rounder. THRESHOLD down until the needle moves, RATIO (2 - 4 gentle, 8+ hard), KNEE
  (higher = smoother), ATTACK (slower lets the punch through), RELEASE or AUTO REL,
  AUTO GAIN gives back about half of what it takes. SIDECHAIN HPF stops the bass pumping it.
  TAIL LINGER eases the release out slowly at the end; CHARACTER WARM adds low-end weight as it works.

HITMAN (dual FET peak compressor)
  Turn INPUT up to compress harder, OUTPUT to set the level (OUTPUT also drives SATURATION).
  ATTACK and RELEASE: 7 is fastest. RATIO 4 / 8 / 12 / 20, or ALL for the aggressive, crunchy smash.
  MODE L/R or M/S; LINK makes channel 2 follow channel 1's knobs (turn it off to set them apart).
  LOOKAHEAD (up to 2 ms) catches the very first edge of a hit. SC EQ shapes what it listens to
  (e.g. SC HPF so the kick doesn't pump it); LISTEN lets you hear that. SOURCE EXT uses the
  sidechain input in a DAW (the rack has no second input, so there it listens to itself).
  SATURATION: DRY, CLIP (nothing over 0 dBFS), WARM, TRIODE, TAPE. MIX for parallel smashing.
  The screen on the right shows the last few seconds: gain reduction of both channels and the input.

SWITCHBLADE
  A compressor and a gate on one plate. Compressor: THRESHOLD, RATIO, ATTACK, RELEASE (or AUTO REL),
  MAKEUP. Gate: GATE OPEN = the level it opens at (all the way down = off), CLOSE BELOW = how far
  under that it shuts (stops chatter), GATE RANGE = how far down it goes, HOLD, GATE REL.
  FILTER: KEY = only what the detector hears (e.g. HIGH-PASS so the kick doesn't pump it);
  BAND = only that band of the sound is processed. LISTEN plays what the detector hears.

TERRITORY
  Each band has THRESHOLD, RANGE and GAIN. RANGE minus = that band comes down when it's loud,
  RANGE plus = it gets lifted when it's loud, 0 = no movement (GAIN still works as an EQ).
  SPLIT 1-4 move the borders. PHASE LINEAR splits the bands with no phase shift (more latency).
  LISTEN plays one band alone. The windows on top show each band's move right now.

CONSIGLIERE
  Two knobs: PEAK REDUCTION (how much) and GAIN (make the level back up). OPTO is smooth and slow,
  SOLID is quicker and firmer. LIMIT for harder control, EMPHASIS so it reacts more to the highs.

HEAVYWEIGHT
  THRESHOLD, RATIO, ATTACK, RELEASE as usual (it makes up the level by itself). PUNCH lets the hits
  through while the body is held. ANALOG adds weight and grit. RELEASE ON THE BEAT ties the release to
  the song's tempo (TEMPO FROM HOST in a DAW; BPM knob in the rack). LIMIT keeps peaks under 0 dBFS.

THE DON
  The old broadcast bus sound: it listens to its own output, so it breathes. THRESHOLD down until
  the needle moves, pick RATIO, ATTACK and DECAY (AUTO follows the music). WARMTH for the output stage.

SLOW BURN
  Tube limiting that gets firmer the harder you push. INPUT and THRESHOLD set how much, TIME 1-6
  sets the speed (5 and 6 let go by themselves). LINK: LINKED, DUAL (each side on its own) or
  MID / SIDE. TUBE for colour.

IRON FIST
  Like a console channel: turn INPUT up to compress harder, OUTPUT to set the level. RATIO, ATTACK,
  RECOVERY (AUTO follows the music). LIMITER + LIMIT LEVEL catch the peaks, DE-ESS makes it react to
  esses, IRON is the output transformer.

KNOCKOUT
  ATTACK + makes hits snap, - softens them. SUSTAIN + brings up the ring and room, - dries it.
  SENSITIVITY and LENGTH for each, SHAPE for the feel. MODE 4-BAND lets you set how much each band
  gets (LOW .. HIGH). GUARD CLIP / LIMIT keeps it under 0 dBFS.

FULL HOUSE
  GATE keeps quiet noise down. LOW THRESHOLD + MAX LIFT bring up the quiet parts. LEVELER rides the
  average level (SOFT slower, LOUD faster). HIGH LEVEL holds the loud parts and makes the level back up.

WHEELMAN
  Pick VOCAL or BASS, set TARGET to the level you want. It rides up to MAX BOOST and down to MAX CUT,
  and holds still for anything under SENSITIVITY (gaps, breaths). SPEED SLOW is smoother.

BIG SHOT
  THRESHOLD down = louder; CEILING = nothing comes out above it. BANDS 5 or 16 shares the limiting out
  so the loudest part gives the most; LOW / MID / HIGH KEEPS = which part keeps its punch.
  STYLE CLEAN / PUNCHY / WARM. DITHER 16 BIT when the file goes to 16 bit.

SILENCER
  THRESHOLD down until the esses get caught, RANGE = the most it takes off. DETECT SMART catches loud
  and soft esses the same; CLASSIC listens with a filter at FREQUENCY. TAKES DOWN SPLIT = only the
  highs, WIDE = the whole sound. LISTEN plays what it reacts to.

BREATHLESS
  LEVEL GATE: breaths are quieter than this. SENSITIVITY: how breath-like a sound must be.
  REDUCTION: how far they go down. FADE OUT / IN for smooth edges. LISTEN BREATHS plays only what it takes out.

TOMMY GUN
  One knob: AMOUNT. RATE = how often it pumps (a note of the tempo), OFFSET shifts where in the beat
  it dips. TEMPO FROM HOST in a DAW; BPM knob in the rack.

STRAIGHT SHOOTER
  An EQ that never shifts the phase: good on the mix bus and for mastering. The top row picks the
  frequencies, the row under it the gains and the bell widths (Q). LOW CUT / HIGH CUT SLOPE switch the
  cuts on. It delays the sound by 96 ms; DAWs make up for that by themselves, in the rack you won't notice.

CUTTHROAT
  Turn SWEEP left and the highs close down, right and the lows thin out (filter rides, builds,
  breakdowns). LOW CUT / HIGH CUT are where the filters rest. RESONANCE adds the whistle at the edge,
  SLOPE 24 dB is steeper, DRIVE dirties it up first.

THE BOSS
  A whole channel on one plate. GATE (all the way left = off), then the compressor (THRESHOLD, RATIO,
  ATTACK, RELEASE), then the EQ row. ORDER: EQ FIRST or DYN FIRST. LIMIT keeps the output under -0.3 dB.

KINGPIN
  For drums. PUNCH brings the hits forward, CRUSH mixes in a smashed copy under them (bigger room,
  fatter body), DRIVE saturates. THUMP / SNAP boost the low hit and the crack at their frequencies.

GOLD CHAIN
  One knob: TONE. Turn it up for a warmer, thicker, shinier sound. INPUT pushes harder; with LINK on,
  the output comes down by the same amount so the level stays put.

IVORIES
  For piano and keys. TONE darker or brighter, BODY for the low-mid weight, COMPRESS to even it out.
  DELAY adds a soft stereo slap (TIME), DOUBLER two drifting copies left and right, WIDTH 0..200 %.

HIGH ROLLER
  Five bands: SUB, LOW, MID, HIGH, AIR. LIFT brings up the detail in that band (quiet parts more
  than loud ones). WIDTH spreads that band (100 % = as it was, 0 = mono). Try AIR WIDTH 150 %.

THE MIDDLEMAN
  CENTER and SIDES turn the middle (vocal, kick, bass) and the sides up or down. LOW / HIGH BALANCE
  do it only under 200 Hz / over 4 kHz (plus = toward the middle). PUNCH sends the hits to the middle.

DOUBLE CROSS
  Makes a mono sound stereo, and it still sounds right in mono. SPREAD = how wide. STYLE = how many
  pieces it's split into. LOW STAYS MONO keeps the bass in the middle.

THE FIXER
  Three bands that only turn down when they get nasty: MUD (boxy lows), HARSH (the bite) and FIZZ
  (esses, hiss). Bring a band's THRESHOLD down until its number shows a few dB; RANGE = the most it
  takes. LISTEN plays one band alone so you can find the right FREQ.

SIX SHOOTER
  SHOTS = how many echoes in a row, NOTE = how far apart (FREE uses TIME in ms). DECAY makes each
  shot quieter, SPREAD bounces them left / right, SWING makes every second one late, FEEDBACK starts
  the round again, TONE darkens them.

BACK ALLEY
  The cheap-room sound: SPACE SPRING or PLATE, REVERB and ECHO levels, DECAY of the room, TIME and
  FEEDBACK of the echo. WOBBLE bends the echo like old tape, GRIT narrows and dirties it, NARROW
  squeezes the whole thing toward mono.

DRIVE-BY
  MANUAL: turn POSITION and the sound moves past you (move it fast and the pitch bends). AUTO: it
  drives past on its own, once per PASS EVERY. SPEED = how fast (how much the pitch bends), DISTANCE =
  how close it passes, AIR = far away gets darker.

BODY DOUBLE
  Makes one take sound like two (or four). DELAY = how late the copies are, DETUNE = how out of tune,
  SPREAD = how far left / right. VOICE LEVEL and DRY balance them; LOW CUT keeps the copies out of the bass.

HUSTLE
  One wobble for three things: TREMOLO (level), VIBRATO (pitch) and PAN. RATE in Hz, or pick a NOTE
  to lock it to the tempo. WAVE changes the shape; OFFSET moves where the pan swings.

THE INFORMANT
  Talk or sing into it and the built-in synth "speaks" your words. NOTE, OCTAVE and CHORD set what
  the synth plays, CARRIER its sound. BANDS 32 = clearer words. SHIFT moves the voice character up
  or down, SIBILANCE lets the S and T sounds through.

STRAPPED
  Everything a guitar needs in the mix, top row first: TIGHT (low cut), COMPRESS, BASS, MIDS, TREBLE;
  then CHORUS, DELAY (DELAY NOTE follows the tempo), ROOM and WIDTH.

SAFE HOUSE
  For checking a mix on headphones: it sounds like speakers in front of you. ANGLE = how wide they
  sit, CROSSFEED = how much each ear hears the other speaker, ROOM DRY / STUDIO / LIVE and ROOM LEVEL.
  It is for listening only: switch it off before you export.

FURNACE
  DRIVE for more tube colour (the level stays about the same), BIAS toward EVEN = warmer.
  TONE darker or brighter after the tube, MIX to blend.

CATHEDRAL, CHROME
  DECAY = how many seconds the tail lasts. PRE-DELAY keeps the dry sound clear before the
  reverb starts (20 - 60 ms on vocals). DAMPING makes the tail darker. LOW CUT on the reverb
  keeps the low end clean. For a send-style sound in the rack, keep MIX low (10 - 25 %).

REPEAT OFFENDER
  Pick a NOTE (1/4, 1/8, dotted, triplets) and set BPM to your song's tempo, or FREE and TIME in
  ms. PING-PONG bounces the repeats left and right. LOW / HIGH CUT shape the repeats.

THE CREW, GETAWAY, SMOKESCREEN, SHAKEDOWN, CYCLONE, SHAPESHIFTER
  RATE = how fast, DEPTH = how much. MIX 50 % gives GETAWAY and SMOKESCREEN their deepest sweep.
  CYCLONE: switch SLOW / FAST and listen to it speed up - the horn gets there before the drum.
  SHAPESHIFTER: ENVELOPE + opens the filter when the music gets louder (funk), - closes it.

UNDERGROUND
  OCTAVE: a new sub an octave under the bass. HARMONICS: the bass's overtones, so the low end
  comes through on phones and laptops. Start with AMOUNT low - it adds up fast.

SPOTLIGHT
  Top to bottom of a vocal chain: HIGH-PASS, BODY, COMPRESS (one knob, with makeup), DE-ESS
  (one knob), PRESENCE, AIR. Everything at 0 = untouched.

THE ACCOUNTANT (measures only, changes nothing)
  INTEGRATED = the whole song since RESET (play it all the way through). SHORT-TERM = the last
  3 seconds, MOMENTARY = the last 0.4 s. LOUDNESS RANGE = how much it moves (LU).
  TRUE PEAK MAX = the highest peak so far. TARGET: -14 (Spotify / YouTube), -16 (Apple Music),
  -11 / -9 (loud); TO TARGET shows how far off you are (+ = louder than the target).

Knobs: drag up / down (hold Shift for fine), mouse wheel, double-click = back to default.

OUTPUT (top right) picks the sound card. HEM-Elite-Rack uses it in exclusive mode
(lowest latency) — other programs go quiet while it plays.

Your chain, output and folders are remembered in %APPDATA%\HEM-Elite-Rack.
The VST3 plug-ins (all of them, named HEM ...) are in Common Files\VST3\HEM-Elite-Rack.vst3
for any DAW that loads VST3.

Needs: Windows 10 / 11 64-bit, a processor with AVX2 (Intel 2013+, AMD 2015+), OpenGL 3.3.
