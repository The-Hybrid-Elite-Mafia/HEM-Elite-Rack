HEM-ELITE-RACK - FACEPLATE TEMPLATES
====================================

One picture per plug-in, 2560 x 1441, in a folder per category: EQ, DYNAMICS, COLOR, SPACE,
MOVEMENT, TOOLS, KEYS. Each picture is the plug-in's faceplate exactly as the app draws it today,
with every knob, button, switch, screen and meter in its real place.

WHAT THE NEW ART IS FOR
The app keeps drawing the working parts (knobs, buttons, switches, screens, meters, keyboards).
The new picture is the metal plate around them. When a picture comes back, each control's pocket is
cut out of it and the real control is set into it.

RULES FOR THE NEW PICTURE (FOR CHATGPT OR ANY ARTIST)
1. Same shape: 16:9 landscape (1672 x 941 is the plate's own ratio). 4K or 8K is fine.
2. Every control stays exactly where it is and the same size. Do not move, add, remove or resize
   any knob, button, switch, screen, meter or key. Give each one a clean recess or well to sit in.
3. Words: copy every word exactly as it is on the template (plug-in name, control names, the
   HYBRID ELITE MAFIA and HEM-ELITE-RACK branding). If a word comes out wrong it is fixed later, but
   get as close as possible.
4. Never use another company's name, product name, logo or trademark anywhere.
5. No glow, bloom or haze around lights or edges. Lights are solid. The plate is metal, not neon.
6. Keep the crown badge top left and the hooded ghost top right.
7. Keep the file name the same (HEM_<NAME>.png) so each picture finds its plug-in.

WHERE THEY GO
Finished pictures go into the Google Drive folder "Plate Vault", in the folder for the same category.
