## Description
HEM-Elite-Rack is a plug-in rack built from the ground up by Hybrid Elite Mafia.

Open a song (WAV, MP3 or FLAC), stack up to 8 plug-ins in the chain, and hear every move as you make it. Flip
between your processed mix and the original with A / B — with MATCH LOUDNESS, so louder never fools your ears —
then export the finished WAV.

120 plug-ins, each with its own custom faceplate:
• EQ — from surgical 16-band mastering EQ to warm passive tone
• DYNAMICS — compressors, limiters, transient shapers, de-essers, gates
• COLOR — tape, tube, saturation, lo-fi
• SPACE — reverbs and delays
• MOVEMENT — chorus, flanger, phaser, tremolo, filters
• TOOLS — meters, loudness, stereo, analyzers
• KEYS — a playable instrument

Built for real time:
• Its own audio engine, written in C++ from scratch — no borrowed framework
• ASIO and WASAPI for low-latency playback and live input
• Live input and recording: play through the chain and record the take
• Faceplates drawn by your graphics card, so the processor stays free for sound
