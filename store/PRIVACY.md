# HEM-Elite-Rack — Privacy Policy

**Hybrid Elite Mafia** · applies to HEM-Elite-Rack from the Microsoft Store and from the HEM installer.

## What the app collects
**Nothing.** HEM-Elite-Rack does not collect, store, send or sell any personal information. It has no accounts,
no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

## What stays on your computer
- **Your settings** (the plug-in chain, knob positions, which view you like) are saved in a file on your own
  computer so the app opens the way you left it. They never leave your computer.
- **Songs you open** are read from where you keep them. **Exports and recordings** are saved only where you
  choose. The app doesn't copy or send them anywhere.

## Microphone / sound-card inputs
When you turn on **LIVE INPUT** or **RECORD**, the app listens to the sound-card input you pick so you can play
through the plug-ins and record takes. That sound is processed on your computer and saved only if you record,
to the folder you choose. Windows asks your permission first, and you can turn it off any time in
Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone.

## Children
The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
